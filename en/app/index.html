Mijia / Xiaomi Home app

The app is optional for everyday use. It provides Custom mode, usage statistics, and reminders.

Pairing

  1. Install Mijia / Xiaomi Home from your app store or use the QR code in the original manual.
  2. Open the app and tap “+” in the upper-right corner.
  3. Follow the prompts to add the device.
  4. When the app detects the water flosser, press and hold the upper power button to pair it over Bluetooth.

The interface may change with updates. The photo does not specify how long to hold the button for pairing. This model is intended for mainland China; availability in other app regions has not been checked here.

What you can set up

  • Custom mode: delivery pattern, frequency, and pressure.
  • Reports: session frequency and duration.
  • Reminders: times and days.
  • Travel lock: turn it on or off.
System notification reminders require a compatible Bluetooth gateway. According to a note in the manual, a phone alone is not enough for this function.
