Troubleshooting

Start with the basic checks in the manual’s table. If the device is damaged or has an unusual smell, stop using it.

ProblemWhat to check / do
Battery drains quickly after chargingThe charging time may have been too short. Fully charge the device.
Will not turn onCharge the device. Also check whether Travel lock is on.
Weak streamThe nozzle may be dirty or damaged; replace it. If the water path is blocked, clean the inside of the tank and the path as described in the manual.
Port moisture warning while chargingDisconnect the charger and dry the port.
Unusual display indicationThe temperature is outside the operating range; use the device at room temperature. If the nozzle is blocked, remove the debris.
Water flow stopped unexpectedlyCheck the water level, the 2-minute automatic pause, and whether the tip is pressed too firmly. Briefly press the power button to resume the session.

Safety information from the manual

Charging and electricity
  • Input: 5 V ⎓ 1 A. Charging takes about 4 hours at 0–35 °C.
  • Use the supplied charging cable. The manual limits the power supply to 15 W; output voltage must be 5 V and the source must meet SELV requirements.
  • Do not touch the charger or cable with wet hands.
  • Before charging, dry the body, port, and cable. Do not insert foreign objects into the port.
  • Do not bend, alter, pull, or twist the cable.
  • Do not replace internal wires, components, or protective devices yourself.
Who should take precautions
  • Children and people with limited physical, sensory, or mental abilities, or insufficient experience, should use the device under the supervision of someone responsible for their safety.
  • Do not allow children to play with, clean, or maintain the device without supervision.
  • If you have periodontal disease or have had oral surgery within the last 2 months, use the device only as directed by a dentist.
  • If you have an open sore in your mouth, consult a doctor before use.
  • If discomfort lasts more than 2 weeks, stop using the device and consult a dentist.
Water, nozzle, and handling
  • For oral cleaning only. Do not use without a nozzle.
  • Do not fill with water hotter than 40 °C; it may cause burns.
  • Do not add toothpaste, salt, solid mouthwash tablets, or povidone-iodine mouthwash; they may cause blockages or damage.
  • Do not press the nozzle hard against your teeth or gums.
  • Do not use the device near strong magnetic fields or corrosive substances.
  • Do not disassemble or modify the device. Contact service if it needs repair.
  • If you notice an unusual smell, noise, heat, or malfunction, stop using the device immediately, turn it off, and disconnect the power.
  • Do not store the device in heat or direct sunlight. Drain and dry it after each session.
Battery and disposal

The internal battery is not removable by an ordinary user. Do not throw the device or battery into a fire, heat it, charge it, or store it at high temperatures. Doing so may cause a fire or explosion.

At the end of the device’s life, dispose of it according to local rules for electronics and batteries. The manual provides a separate battery removal procedure for disposal only: disconnect the power, remove the tank, take out the two plugs and two screws on the bottom, remove the bottom cover and port plug, release the two clips, remove the mechanism, and disconnect the battery from the circuit board. Do not turn the device on after removing the battery. Do not short-circuit the battery contacts; dispose of the battery safely.

It is more practical to have a specialist disassemble it: the manual requires tools and cutting connections. The corresponding illustrations are in original photos 3–5.
