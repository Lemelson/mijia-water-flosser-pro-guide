Does a new device need cleaning?
The manual does not require sterilizing the device or using vinegar before first use. Rinsing the nozzle and tank with clean water is a practical hygiene step, not a separate manufacturer requirement. Do not use boiling water, alcohol, or harsh cleaners.

Get ready in 5 steps

1

Charge it first

Charge the device before first use. A full charge takes about 4 hours. The body, port, and cable must be dry.

2

Rinse with water

Use clean water below 40 °C. A small amount of water inside a new device is normal: every unit is tested at the factory.

3

Attach the fan nozzle

Cloud Multi-Effect Nozzle 2 / 云感多效喷嘴 2. Insert it vertically until it clicks.

4

Fill the large tank

Open the top lid, hold the body upright, fill with water below 40 °C, and close the lid.

5

Select 轻柔 — Gentle

Use the lower button to change the mode. Put the nozzle in your mouth and lean over the sink before pressing the upper button.

My first use

Do not start by directing the stream at your face. Turn it on only with the nozzle in your mouth. Keep your lips slightly open so the water can drain freely.
