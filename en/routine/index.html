Everyday use

Hold the body upright. Start at the back teeth and move along the gumline.

Swipe the illustration sideways: attach → fill → use.

1

Fill and close

Fill through the top opening with clean tap water or warm water below 40 °C. Close the lid firmly.

2

Choose a mode

Use the lower button ↻. Beginners should choose 轻柔 and the fan nozzle.

3

Lean over the sink

Put the nozzle in your mouth. Close your lips to prevent splashing, leaving a small gap so water can drain.

4

Turn it on and move slowly

Press the upper button ⏻. Aim the stream at 90° to the gumline. Move from the back teeth forward, pausing briefly between teeth.

5

Pause before removing the nozzle

Stop the water flow with the upper button, then remove the nozzle.

6

Drain and dry

Pour out the water, run the device for a few seconds to clear the remaining water from the path, then stop it. Open the lid and leave the device to dry in a ventilated place.

Use the remaining water and your comfort as a guide.

Do not press the tip against your teeth. Pressing it firmly against your teeth or gums may trigger a protective pause. Briefly press the power button to resume.

Do not aim the stream directly into a gum pocket. Do not use the device without a nozzle. Do not direct water into your eyes, ears, or nose.
