Remove and rinse the nozzle

Rinse it with clean water, dry it, and put it in the storage compartment in the body or in the case. Leave the tank lid open until it is dry.

What you can use to clean it

  • Clean the tank and body with a damp cloth or clean water, then dry them.
  • Water for cleaning — no higher than 40 °C.
  • Use a soft brush to remove scale inside the tank.
  • Do not use acidic liquids for routine cleaning, solvents, alcohol, or abrasive cleaners.
The white vinegar described below is for a separate periodic maintenance procedure. Do not use it for regular mouth rinsing or add it continuously.

Descale every 1–3 months

Procedure from your manual:

  1. Fill the tank with warm water no hotter than 40 °C.
  2. Add one tablespoon of white vinegar. The vinegar concentration and tank volume are not specified in this part of the photo.
  3. Run the device with the nozzle aimed into the sink and wait for the tank to empty.
  4. After it empties, continue running it for about 1 minute until the device turns itself off, as stated in the manual.
  5. Refill it with clean water no hotter than 40 °C and run it until the tank is empty.

This maintenance procedure is performed outside the mouth. If your device behaves differently or you are unsure about the cleaning agent, check with the manufacturer; do not use strong acids.

Long-term storage

Wash and dry the device completely. Fully charge it before long-term storage. The manual says to recharge it at least once every 6 months. Do not store it in heat, direct sunlight, or a damp place.

When to replace the nozzle

Replace it every 3–6 months, or immediately if the tip is deformed. The manual says a deformed nozzle may cause the device to malfunction.
