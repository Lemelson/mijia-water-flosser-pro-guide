轻柔 — gentle

Continuous gentle stream. For beginners and sensitive gums.

Recommended starting point: Gentle mode + Cloud Multi-Effect Nozzle 2. The manual recommends using this combination for one week, then choosing other modes based on how it feels.

Try ⏻ to start / pause or ↻ to change the mode. This is a simulation and is not connected to your device.

Two buttons. Several functions.

⏻ Upper button — power

  • Short press: start or pause water flow.
  • Press and hold for more than 12 seconds: restart the device.
  • When pairing with Mijia: after the app detects the device, press and hold the button to pair over Bluetooth. The photo does not specify how long to hold it.

↻ Lower button — mode

  • Short press: change the mode while the device is off, awake, or running.
  • Press and hold for 3 seconds: open settings while paused or awake.
  • After 12 seconds of inactivity: the device turns off automatically while paused or awake.
Travel lock
  1. While paused, press and hold the lower button for 3 seconds to open settings.
  2. On the 旅行锁 page, briefly press the upper button to turn on the lock.
  3. The buttons and lift-to-wake function are disabled.
  4. To unlock, press and hold either button. The manual does not specify how long to hold it.

You can also turn the lock on or off in Mijia.

Lift to wake

The device wakes when you pick it up while it is off. Frequent repeated activations may temporarily disable this function. Charging the device or briefly pressing a button restores it.

The last mode is saved. When switched on, the device returns to the mode selected before it was last turned off. This simulation shows what the screens mean; menu order and animations may differ on your device.
