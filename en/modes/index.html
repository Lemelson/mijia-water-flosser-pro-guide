How to attach and remove a nozzle

To attach: insert the nozzle vertically into the opening at the top until it clicks. To remove: with the water flow off, press the release button on top of the body while pulling the nozzle upward.

Do not press the release button while the device is running: the nozzle may pop out. Replace the nozzle if its tip is deformed. The recommended replacement interval is every 3–6 months.

How to replace the tank

Illustration from the manual. Remove and install the parts vertically, without tilting them.

How to switch between the small and large tanks

First make sure no water remains. Hold the body with one hand and remove the small tank’s bottom cover by pulling it straight down with the other. Insert the large tank vertically from below until it is fully seated with no gap. To switch back, pull the large tank down and install the small tank cover.

The small tank is built into the body and is intended for travel and pulsed delivery. The removable large tank is for home use and continuous delivery.

The device automatically pauses after 2 minutes of continuous use. To continue, briefly press the upper button. This is a session limit; it does not mean the tank holds enough water for 2 minutes.
